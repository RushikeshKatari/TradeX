Exchange prices are USD per troy ounce. INR mode converts to INR per 10g using `USD/oz × USD/INR × 10 ÷ 31.1034768 × (1 + India adjustment %)`. The 14.12% default is calibrated from your supplied current price comparison; edit it when your local reference basis changes. This adjustment is separate from the USD/INR rate. Candles remain raw USD internally; missing source candles are reported and never interpolated. Sessions overlap; daily totals count each candle once.
